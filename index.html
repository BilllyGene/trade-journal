Sync between devices

Keeps your laptop and phone the same, automatically. It uses a free private GitHub repository as a shared safe for your journal. Every sync is also saved in that repository's history, so it works as an extra backup.

One-time setup (click-by-click steps are in the README, section "Sync"):

  1. On GitHub, create a new private repository named trade-journal-data.
  2. Create a fine-grained access token: only that repository, permission Contents: Read and write.
  3. Fill in below and tap Connect. Then do steps 2 and 3 on your other device, with a new token made for that device.

The token is kept only in this browser on this device. It can open only the one repository you chose.

Disconnecting only removes the token from this device. Your trades stay here and on GitHub.

Guide

Tap a heading to open it. Everything here is about this app. Press Escape or ✕ Close to return to where you were.

1. Quick start: the 60-second routine after every trade
  1. Tap Log trade.
  2. Fill in Outcome, Entry, Stop loss, Take profit and, if the trade is closed, Exit and P&L (copy it from MT5).
  3. Tap Confirm on each checklist step you really saw. Add a chart screenshot to the steps that matter.
  4. Answer Followed my plan? honestly, tap any Mistakes, pick the Emotion, and write one line in Notes.
  5. Tap Save trade. It appears in the Trade log, and the Dashboard updates.

Why straight after the trade: you still remember why you entered and how you felt. A week later the details are gone, and the journal stops teaching you anything.

Blue and yellow boxes

Blue = worked out by the app. Yellow = read from your screenshot (part 11): check it. Typing in a box makes it yours, and the app stops changing it.

Why the estimate might differ from MT5: the app multiplies the price move by your lot size and the contract size (100 oz per lot for gold, 100,000 units for forex). MT5 also takes off commission and swap. For indices or crypto, set "Money per 1.00 price move" in Settings, because brokers differ.

Checklist and screenshots

  • Each step has a Confirm button. Only confirm what you actually saw before entering.
  • + Add screenshots under a step adds chart pictures to that step. You can pick several at once. Pictures are shrunk automatically to save space.
  • Laptop shortcut: take a screenshot (Windows: Win+Shift+S), click the step, then press Ctrl+V. It is pasted into the highlighted step. You can also drag image files onto the box.
  • General screenshots is for anything not tied to a step, such as the MT5 result.
  • Tap a thumbnail to see it full screen. The small × removes it.

Saving

Missing or wrong fields turn red with a message, and nothing you typed is lost. If saving ever fails (for example the phone is full), the form stays as it was and tells you what to do.

Clear form empties everything (it asks first). Switching tabs does not lose what you typed.

3. Open trades: closing them later
  1. Log the trade with Outcome Open when you enter. Exit and P&L can stay empty.
  2. When it closes: Trade log → find it (filter Outcome: Open) → Edit.
  3. Fill Exit, P&L, change Outcome, answer Followed my plan?, then Save trade.

Why open trades are left out of the stats: their result isn't known yet, so counting them would make win rate and P&L wrong. They still count towards your daily and weekly trade limits.

4. Trade log: finding, editing, copying, deleting
  • Newest trades are at the top. Each card shows prices, R:R, result, checklist (e.g. 3/4), plan, mistakes, notes and screenshots grouped by step.
  • Filters: Outcome, Session, Followed plan, Instrument and Date range. They combine: e.g. Loss + Followed plan No + This month shows this month's avoidable losses. Clear filters resets them.
  • Edit opens the trade in the form, with its screenshots and ticks. It keeps its own checklist steps even if you changed your checklist since.
  • Duplicate starts a new trade with the same setup (instrument, direction, prices, risk, grade) and today's date, but no result and no screenshots. Handy for re-entries.
  • Delete asks first. With sync on, the trade is removed from your other device too.
5. Dashboard: what every number means

Closed trades means Win, Loss and Breakeven. Open trades are left out of every result below.

NumberHow it is worked out
Total tradesEvery trade, including open ones.
Win rateWins ÷ (Wins + Losses). Breakevens are left out, so they don't drag the rate down.
Net P&LThe P&L of all closed trades added up.
Profit factorTotal won ÷ total lost. Above 1.0 means you make more than you lose. Shows "—" until you have a loss.
Plan followedShare of closed trades where you answered Yes.
Full-checklist win rateWin rate counting only trades where every step was confirmed. Compare it with your normal win rate: if it is clearly higher, your checklist works, so wait for it.
Balance / ReturnOnly if a starting balance is set in Settings: starting balance + net P&L.
Average win / lossAverage P&L of your winners and of your losers.
Average R:R (planned)The reward you aimed for compared with your risk, averaged over trades that have one.
Average result in RWhat you actually got, in units of risk (only trades with Risk filled). A positive number means you have an edge.
Most common mistakeThe mistake you tick most often: your first thing to fix.

Process vs result: the four boxes

  • Followed plan + Win: a good trade. Repeat it.
  • Followed plan + Loss: a good loss. Losses are part of any strategy; accept it.
  • Broke plan + Win: lucky and dangerous. It rewards bad habits.
  • Broke plan + Loss: a lesson. Check the Mistakes you ticked.

Why this matters most: one trade's result is partly luck, but following the plan is fully in your control. If the "followed plan" boxes grow, results follow over time.

Charts and the rest

  • Equity curve: running total of P&L (or balance, if set) after each closed trade. Hover or tap a point to see that trade.
  • Wins vs losses by session: shows which session works for you and which to avoid.
  • Recent form: your last 20 trades as dots, oldest on the left. Green win, red loss, grey breakeven, outlined open.
  • Show numbers for (appears once you trade 2+ instruments): pick one instrument to see only its numbers and charts.
  • Reminders at the top: your own rules (edit in Settings). The coloured line counts trades today/this week and the money left before your daily loss limit. It turns red when you hit a limit: that's the signal to stop.
6. Settings: making the journal yours
  • Appearance: Light, Dark or Auto (follows the phone or laptop setting).
  • Checklist steps: load preset A (ICC) or B, or write your own 1 to 8 steps, reorder with ↑ ↓, then Save checklist. Old trades keep the steps they were logged with, so your history stays honest when your strategy changes.
  • Main instrument and Account currency symbol: what you trade most, and the money symbol used everywhere.
  • Money per 1.00 price move for 1 lot: only needed if the automatic P&L/Risk estimate is missing or wrong for your main instrument (e.g. indices).
  • Read trades from screenshots: where you save your Claude API key (part 11).
  • Password lock: lock the journal on this device with a password (part 13).
  • Max trades per day/week and Daily loss limit: leave blank to hide. They feed the line under the reminders.
  • Starting balance: turns on Balance and Return, and the equity curve shows your balance.
  • Reminders and Mistake options: one per line. Click Save.
7. Sync: keeping laptop and phone the same

The chip at the top right shows the state:

ChipMeaning
Synced 2 min ago (green)All good.
Syncing…Working. Wait a moment.
Offline (grey)No internet. Keep logging; it catches up by itself.
Sync problem (red)Tap it. The message in Settings says what's wrong, usually an expired token: make a new one and paste it again.
Sync off (dashed)Not set up on this device. Tap it to set up.
  • Sync runs when the app opens, a few seconds after every change, every 5 minutes, and when the internet returns. Sync now in Settings forces it.
  • If the same trade is changed on both devices before they sync, the later change wins. The other version is kept in the GitHub repository's conflicts folder.
  • Your checklist and settings sync too. Light/Dark mode stays per device.
  • Every device needs its own token (see README, part 6).
8. Backups and Excel
  • Export full backup (.json): everything, including screenshots. Keep a copy in Google Drive or email it to yourself now and then. It's your safety net if GitHub or a device ever fails.
  • Export trades to Excel (.csv): one row per trade for your own analysis in Excel or Google Sheets. It has no screenshots and can't be imported back.
  • Import backup (.json): shows a summary first. Merge adds new trades and keeps the newest copy of each. Importing the same file twice never creates duplicates. Replace all wipes this device and uses only the backup (it asks twice).
  • The dashboard reminds you when a backup is overdue. A recent sync counts as a backup.
9. Phone tips
  • Install it: Android Chrome ⋮ → Add to Home screen; iPhone Safari Share → Add to Home Screen. It then opens like an app and works offline.
  • On iPhone, installing also protects your data: Safari can clear data for websites you haven't opened in a while.
  • Use ± next to P&L to enter a loss.
  • To get a new version of the app: close it fully and open it again. Settings → About shows the version.
10. Weekly review: 10 minutes every weekend
  1. Trade log → Date range This week. Read each card and its notes.
  2. Dashboard: look at Process vs result. Count the "broke plan" trades and what they cost.
  3. Check Most common mistake. Pick one thing to fix next week and add it to your Reminders.
  4. Compare Full-checklist win rate with your normal Win rate. If full-checklist trades do better, be stricter about waiting for every step.
  5. Look at Wins vs losses by session. Consider skipping the session that keeps losing.
  6. Export a backup.

Why weekly: single trades are noise. Patterns only show over several trades, and a week is long enough to see one and short enough to fix it quickly.

11. Fill a trade from a screenshot
  1. Take a screenshot showing the trade: MT5's trade or history line, the position details, or the chart with entry, SL and TP lines.
  2. On Log trade, tap 📷 Fill from screenshot and choose it. On a laptop you can instead click the blue box and press Ctrl+V.
  3. Wait 5 to 20 seconds. Empty boxes fill in and turn yellow. Check each one against the screenshot.
  4. Fill what's still missing (checklist, plan, emotion), then Save trade.
  • Already typed some boxes? They are never overwritten. If the screenshot shows something different, the app lists it and offers Use the screenshot's values instead.
  • The screenshot is saved with the trade under General screenshots.
  • It reads only what is visible. Anything it can't see stays empty, and the app works out R:R, risk, P&L and outcome from what it found.

Setting it up (once per device)

Screenshot reading uses Claude, Anthropic's AI, with your own API key: Settings → Read trades from screenshots has the steps. Each screenshot costs roughly 2 to 4 US cents from the credit on your Anthropic account. The key stays on that device only.

Why it can be wrong: blurry or cropped pictures, or several trades on one screen, can confuse it. That's why boxes it fills are yellow, and why your own typing always wins.

13. Password lock and getting back in

What it protects: anyone using your phone or laptop. People you send the link to never see your trades anyway: trades live only in your browser and your private sync storage, so they get an empty journal of their own.

Turn it on

  1. Settings → Password lock: type a password twice (at least 6 characters), add a hint if you like, then Set password.
  2. A recovery code appears, like ABCD-EFGH-JKLM-NPQR. Save it: Copy it into a note in your email, Save as a file, or write it on paper. It's shown only once.
  3. Do the same on your other devices. Each device has its own lock; you can use the same password on each.

Every day

  • The journal opens to the lock screen. Enter the password to see anything.
  • It locks again when you've been away for the time you choose (Settings: from "any time I leave the app" to 1 hour; the default is 5 minutes). The 🔒 button at the top locks it at once.
  • After 5 wrong tries it makes you wait, longer each time.

Change it, or if you forget it

  • Change password: Settings → Password lock → Change password (asks for the current one). Your recovery code keeps working.
  • Forgot the password: lock screen → Show my hint, or Forgot password? → enter the recovery code and a new password. You then get a new recovery code to save (the used one stops working).
  • Lost the recovery code but still know the password: Settings → New recovery code.
  • Lost both: lock screen → Forgot password? → Lost the recovery code too? This erases the journal on this device only and removes the lock. Your trades come back from sync (reconnect with a new GitHub token) or from a backup file.

Why there's no "email me a reset link": the journal has no server: everything stays on your devices. That's also why the recovery code matters: keep it somewhere safe that isn't this device.

Good to know: the lock hides the journal, but it doesn't encrypt the data stored in the browser. A determined expert with your unlocked computer could still dig into the browser's files. For full protection, also lock the phone or laptop itself.
